The ledger

A receipt nobody can quietly change

Client names and amounts stay private in my database. Only a fingerprint of each receipt goes on the chain, which is enough to prove the receipt has not been altered since the day it was issued.

See the ledger Verify a receipt

Signed

Each receipt's SHA-256 fingerprint is signed with my private key (ECDSA, secp256k1).

Mined into a block

The signed transaction is sealed into a proof-of-work block, linked by hash to every block before it.

Checked by anyone

Scan the QR code on a receipt and this site re-checks the hash, signature, Merkle proof and chain.
