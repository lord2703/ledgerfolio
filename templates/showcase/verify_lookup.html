What gets checked

The data

The receipt's details are hashed again and compared with the fingerprint taken when it was issued.

The signature

The fingerprint on the blockchain must be signed by {{ owner_name }}'s wallet, and by no one else's.

The chain

A Merkle proof shows the transaction is in its block, and the whole chain is re-validated back to its first block.
